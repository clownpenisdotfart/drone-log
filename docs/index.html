About

Most drones in the US must broadcast a public Remote ID signal with their position, height and speed. Community receivers around town pick up those broadcasts and this map shows them.

Receivers only listen. They never transmit to or interfere with drones. Only aircraft are shown: no phones, cars or people are tracked, and pilot locations are not published.

Some aircraft, including some public-safety drones, may not broadcast Remote ID, and receivers only cover part of the city. A quiet map does not mean nothing is flying.

Have a log from a handheld receiver? Open it here; it stays in your browser.
